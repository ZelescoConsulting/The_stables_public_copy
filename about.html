About The Stables

A place to create, capture and be inspired.

A boutique photography studio in Deepdene, Melbourne.

Our story

Style with substance.

The Stables of Melbourne was founded by author and consultant Lucia Zelesco. Lucia believes in blending style with substance, and created The Stables as a space where photographers, brands and creatives can bring their ideas to life.

The Stables is more than a creative studio. It's a movement. Born from a passion for inclusion, empowerment and expression, we welcome all nationalities, genders and diversities.
