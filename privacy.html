The Stables of Melbourne

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Introduction

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how The Stables of Melbourne (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you visit our website, book a tour or a space, send us a message, join our mailing list, share your story with us, or come to one of our events. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Information we collect

We only collect the information we need. Depending on how you use our website and services, this may include:

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, the topic and message you send, how you'd like us to reply, and a phone number only if you choose to give one.
  • Bookings: your name, email address, the tour or space and time you choose, and any notes you add.
  • Mailing list: your email address, and whether you open or click our emails.
  • Stories: what you choose to tell us when you share your story, including your name (or that you'd like to stay anonymous), your contact details, and your permission to share it.
  • Emails you send us directly.
  • Website visits: basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser and device, which is recorded automatically by the services that deliver our website.

You don't have to give us your name to browse the website, and you can ask a general question without telling us who you are. We may need your name and email address to take a booking or reply to you.

Stories and sensitive information

Some of the stories people share with us include personal experiences, such as health, identity or wellbeing. We treat this information with particular care. We only publish a story, a name or a photo with the clear permission of the person who shared it, we respect requests to stay anonymous or to use a first name only, and you can ask us to change or remove your story at any time.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • reply to your enquiries and manage tours, studio hire and events;
  • send you news about the studio, events and workshops, if you have subscribed;
  • share community stories, with permission;
  • keep our website working, secure and easy to use.

We will never sell your information, and we don't use it to make automated decisions about you.

Services we use

Like most small businesses, we use trusted online services to run our website, forms and bookings. They store or process information on our behalf, under their own privacy policies. At the moment we use:

  • Web3Forms, to send us the messages you write in our enquiry form.
  • Cal.com, to take tour and studio bookings and send booking confirmations and reminders.
  • Kit, to manage our mailing list and send our newsletter.
  • Tally, to collect the stories you share with us.
  • GitHub Pages, to host our website.
  • Google services, including Google Fonts (to display our website's lettering), Google Drive (to play some of our videos) and our Google account (email and calendar).
  • ImprovMX, to forward emails sent to our website's email address.
  • Pexels, which supplies some of the photos on our website.
  • Video platforms, such as YouTube, Vimeo, Instagram, TikTok or Facebook, when you choose to play a video shown on our website. Those platforms may collect information about you, under their own policies.

From time to time we may change or add providers that do the same jobs, for example Mailchimp for our mailing list, Calendly for bookings, Formspree for our forms, a service that shows our Instagram photos on the website, a payment provider to take booking payments, or a privacy-friendly visitor statistics tool. Whichever provider we use, we only share the information that service needs, and we will update this list when our services change.

Information stored overseas

Many of these services store information on servers outside Australia, mainly in the United States and the European Union. When we use them, your information may be stored or processed in those countries. We choose established providers that protect personal information, and we only send them what they need to do their job.

Photos and filming at the studio and our events

We may take photos or film at our events to share on our website and social media. We'll let you know when this is happening, and if you'd rather not appear, just tell us on the day or contact us afterwards and we'll remove you.

Unsubscribing

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You can unsubscribe at any time, and we will stop sending you marketing emails.

How long we keep your information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the reasons above, or as the law requires. After that, we delete it or remove anything that identifies you.

Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access, change or disclosure, including by using secure, password-protected services. However, no method of internet transmission or storage is completely secure.

Cookies

Our website itself does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The services above, such as booking calendars, forms and video players, may use cookies or similar technology when you use them, under their own policies. You can change your browser settings to block or delete cookies, though some features may then not work.

Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it or delete it. We will reply within 30 days. There is no charge to make a request.

Questions and complaints

If you have a question or a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please send us a message. We will look into it and reply within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our reply, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or practices change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
