The Stables of Melbourne

We see you. We hear you. You matter.

A place to create, capture and be inspired.

A heritage photography studio in Deepdene, with rooms full of character and soft natural light.

It's also a creative home for the black sheep, the misfits, the golden children, the marginalised and the high achievers. Come as you are, be celebrated for who you are, and share your truth.
